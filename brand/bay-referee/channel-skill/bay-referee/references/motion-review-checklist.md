# Motion review checklist

Use for visual preflight and delivery, with the current task's scope and `motion-grammar.md`. Record PASS / REVISE / NOT APPLICABLE with evidence, not a blanket approval.

1. **Authority:** final voice hash, Whisper map, caption plan, wording/label audit and brand assets identified; old master protected; no renderer replacement. V1.1 visible choreography is the production reference; V1.2 full choreography is not a default. Motion Lab is closed and VAR/SAOT is not rerendered.
2. **Semantic plan:** SCRIPT → FINAL VOICE → WHISPER → SEMANTIC BEATS → VISUAL STATES → MOTION PLAN is designed before rendering, not added to a finished new episode. Every state names spoken range, purpose, source object, inherited state, patterns, focal event, text, avatar, sound/silence, hold and exit. Random pattern selection is absent.
3. **Continuity:** compare last outgoing/first incoming frames. Which object survives? Did position, selected fact, velocity, attachment or camera jump without a reason? Could the existing object form the next state?
4. **Causality:** source precedes reaction; route begins at source; line is anchored; receiving object is readable before a handoff. Don't let a persuasive animation imply an unverified football rule.
5. **Budget:** inspect overlapping animation intervals, not just event counts. One principal action, at most one support, optional type; no competing avatar/area/player/highlight. Remove anything that only fills empty space.
6. **Type:** CAPTION calm/full speech; EDITORIAL TEXT exact authorized phrase; LABEL identifies its object. Single semantic emphasis, separate from role coding. Exits/entrances separately scheduled. No karaoke or SaaS pill clutter.
7. **Host:** every pose change has a target/cause. Stable pose allowed. No facial/hand morphs or claimed independent gaze from a rigid PNG. Captions and targets remain clear.
8. **Material/camera:** object behavior suits its material. Apply only useful conditional physical principles; preparation, follow-through and pose-apex cuts are not compulsory. Timing, curves and distances fit the current material and narration, not hard-coded V1.2 recipes. After an ownership change, verify that the receiver's actual parent/local transform carries its content. Camera motion has a hierarchy/reveal/detail/scale purpose. No arbitrary zoom, generic spring, floating or decorative effect dependence.
9. **Holds:** flag narration-active intervals longer than roughly 2–3s for explanation. Reading, understanding and consequence holds are valid. Never fill them with arbitrary movement.
10. **Media.** Check that the footage and photographs selected for each critical moment appear in the edit and that cropping preserves their meaning. A diagram-only explanatory shot may be marked N/A for this shot-level check. The episode still requires the real coverage defined in [MEDIA-FIRST PRODUCTION](../SKILL.md#media-first-production).
11. **Evidence:** state strip; critical transition consecutive frames with timestamps; full 1080×1920 representatives; motion playback. For revisions, expose a transition loop or 6–12 timestamped side-by-side sequence frames per test, not just posters. Inspect at mobile scale and native size. Document what was actually viewed/listened to.
12. **Build:** repeat/shuffle timestamp hashes; integer-frame coverage; aspect/fps/frame count; audio sameness or authorized audio change; caption geometry/wording; no missing media; original hashes unchanged.
13. **Execution status:** report `selected_patterns`, `executed_patterns`, `rejected_patterns` and `RESEARCHED_BUT_NOT_USED`. Each executed entry names scene/time, visible result and evidence; for revisions, why it improves the comparison. Planned names, minor opacity tweaks or unverified code are not execution proof. Unused research does not need an invented production use.
14. **1× relationship gate:** review the actual planned ownership, hierarchy, source/reaction or role change at normal speed. For revisions, compare against the approved version; a claimed visible choreography improvement fails if indistinguishable. Technical polish with a small perceptual difference must be reported as such, not inflated into a creative gain or used to justify more effects. Style drift or harmed readability fails. New episodes do not require recreating a VAR/SAOT A/B test. A screenshot or slow-motion inspection cannot pass this gate; human approval remains separate.

## Human review — ask in the review surface

- Is B more professionally motion-designed than A?
- Does it still feel like BAY REFEREE?
- Is it more active without becoming crowded?
- Does movement explain the relationship or merely decorate it?
- Do transitions originate from existing objects?
- Is the avatar better integrated?
- Is typography more deliberate?
- Is the reference influence abstract rather than copied?
- Does any section feel like an AI showreel?
- Which choices helped this story and which should be omitted next time? Reusable physical principles remain conditional, not obligations for every scene.

Separate automated QA, internal editorial judgment and human approval. A passing render does not answer these taste questions. Stop at the user's requested review status; no unsolicited full episode or publication.
